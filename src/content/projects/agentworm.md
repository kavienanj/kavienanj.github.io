AgentWorm explores how a lightweight local agent and an LLM-driven C2 brain can work together to simulate reconnaissance, lateral movement, credential discovery, and multi-host coordination in a controlled and observable setting. This project is a research effort intended for academic, defensive, and experimentation purposes only.
